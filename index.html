Our Story

Back in 2017, with my elder brother's support, I set up two chairs and a single mirror near the Adivasi Museum — today The Style Zone has grown into a full unisex studio for hair, skin and bridal beauty.

What hasn't changed is how we treat every client. Whether you're a student in for a ₹150 haircut or a bride planning her big day, you get the same attention, clean tools and proper professional products — not shortcuts.

We keep learning the newer techniques — Keratin, X-Tenso, Smart Bond — so Chhindwara doesn't have to travel to a bigger city for salon-quality treatments. That's the whole idea behind The Style Zone: premium care, close to home.
